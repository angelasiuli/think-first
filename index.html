    How it works

    1. Step 1You decide first

      You write your opinion before the AI says anything, so the idea starts with you.

    2. Step 2The AI pushes back

      It asks one question at a time and gives the other side, like a debate partner.

    3. Step 3You write it

      You write the essay. The AI can comment on your sentences but can't write them.

    Questions

    What is this for?

    For students writing an argument essay. Most AI tools will write the essay if you ask. This one helps you work out what you think and defend it, which is the skill an essay is meant to build.

    Why does it matter who does the thinking?

    Forming your own view and defending it is how you learn to make decisions and lead. In a study of 77 students writing essays, almost half of those who used AI asked it for help before typing a single word. The more of the essay the AI wrote, the less the students felt the essay was theirs.

    Why do I have to write my opinion first?

    Once you read an AI's answer, it's hard to think of your own. Writing one sentence first means the starting idea is yours, and the AI's job becomes testing that idea.

    What if I ask it to write my essay?

    It says no and asks you a question instead. A second check in the code also hides any reply that looks like essay text, so even a clever request won't get you a ready-made essay.

    What does "written by you" mean?

    It's the share of your essay that you typed here. Text you paste in from somewhere else lowers it. It isn't a grade. It just shows how much of the essay is yours.
